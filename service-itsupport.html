What is Managed IT Support?

Proactive IT Management

Managed IT Support means you have a dedicated team monitoring your systems 24/7, fixing issues before they affect your business, and providing expert assistance whenever you need it.

Unlike reactive support that only responds to problems, managed support is proactive—preventing issues through continuous monitoring, updates, and maintenance.
